## Our Fabrication Process From Measurement to Installation

- The Consultation. Our professional design team evaluates your specific space takes exact architectural measurements and helps you select premium stain resistant performance fabrics.
- Master Fabrication. Our skilled artisans construct the solid wood structural framework and complete the bespoke upholstery in our dedicated local workroom. We quality check every single stitch before release.
- Professional Installation. Our vetted installation experts securely mount the custom seating to your walls on your schedule. We coordinate with your other trades if required and ensure your room is left completely spotless.
