## Solar Shades, Glare, and the Modern Office

The single most requested commercial product we install is the solar shade, and for good reason: the modern office is full of screens, and a wall of unshaded west- or south-facing glass turns half of them unreadable by mid-afternoon. A commercial solar shade, chosen at the right openness factor, cuts that glare and a large share of the solar heat gain while preserving the outward view that makes a space feel open. The payoff is not only comfort. Employees stop fighting the blinds, nobody is migrating to the shaded side of the floor, and the cooling system carries a lighter load through Florida's long warm season, which shows up on the utility bill. In conference rooms and presentation spaces, motorized shades on a switch or a schedule let a room shift from daylight to screen-ready in seconds. For patios, courtyards, and outdoor dining, we also fit [outdoor window shades](/services/outdoor-window-shades/) rated for sun and weather. We assess each elevation of the building, west and south glass usually needs a tighter weave while north and east can run more open, and spec the shade per exposure rather than blanketing the building in one fabric, so every workspace gets the right balance of view, light, and heat control.
