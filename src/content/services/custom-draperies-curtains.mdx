## Types of Custom Draperies and Curtains We Make in St. Petersburg

- **Sheer curtains.** Lightweight panels that diffuse Florida sun while keeping the room open and bright. They pair perfectly with heavier drapery you can draw for privacy or darkness.
- **Blackout curtains.** Lined and interlined to block nearly all light for bedrooms, nurseries, and media rooms. Because they are measured to your window, they seal at the sides instead of gapping like off-the-rack panels.
- **Luxury and designer drapery.** Rich fabrics, custom headings, and detailed hardware, from clean modern panels to traditional treatments with contrast borders. This is where a room comes together.
- **Motorized drapery.** Wall switch, remote, app, or voice control for tall windows, sliders, and whole-home automation. We install and configure it so it works from day one.
- **Modern flat panels.** Crisp, minimal drapery on concealed or low-profile tracks for newer and contemporary homes.
- **Layered treatments.** Drapery over [plantation shutters](/services/plantation-shutters/) or [Roman and roller shades](/services/window-shades/), the layered look we build most for formal Snell Isle and Old Northeast rooms.

## Fabrics, Linings & What You Are Actually Buying

The fabric is most of the look and most of the cost, and the lining is what makes drapery last in Florida.

- **Designer face fabrics.** Linen and linen blends, cotton velvets, embroidered sheers, and performance weaves from Kravet, Stout, and partner houses.
- **Linings and interlinings.** Standard, blackout, and thermal-suppression linings; interlining for body and insulation; UV-rated linings for west and Gulf-facing glass.
- **Headings.** Pinch pleat, ripple fold, grommet, and rod pocket, chosen for the room and the hardware.

## Drapery for Sliders, Tall Windows & Specialty Openings

Not every window is a standard rectangle, and the tricky ones are where custom really earns its keep.

- **Sliding glass doors.** Panels on a track that stack clear of the glass when open, with hidden or decorative hardware, standard on Gulf-facing lanais.
- **Two-story and great-room windows.** Long drops handled with the right weight, lining, and motorized control so you are not on a ladder.
- **Bay and bow windows.** Tracks and rods bent to the angles so panels wrap the curve cleanly.
- **Hardware to match.** We also make and install [drapery hardware](/services/drapery-hardware/), decorative rods, rings, finials, and concealed tracks, so the whole treatment is one coordinated package.
