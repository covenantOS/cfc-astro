## Wallpaper brands and papers we sell

We are a St. Pete studio with trade access, not a big-box wallpaper aisle. The books we open most often sit next to the fabrics we already sew.

- **Kravet and our designer houses.** The same trade lines we use for drapery and upholstery also print wallpaper. If you liked a Kravet cloth, we can look for a paper that belongs in the same room.
- **Grasscloth and natural weaves.** Dining rooms, studies, and powder rooms where you want texture more than a printed pattern. Seams show on purpose. That is the material.
- **Vinyl and vinyl-coated wallcovering.** Baths, laundry rooms, rentals, and any wall that gets wiped. This is the paper that handles Florida humidity.
- **Printed papers and murals.** Large repeats and feature walls behind a bed or dining table. Most first jobs start on one wall.
- **Commercial wallcovering.** Offices, waiting rooms, and restaurants that need a wipeable vinyl, not a hobby paper.

If you walked in with a page from another book, bring it. We will tell you whether we can order that line or whether a paper we already sell gets you closer.

## How we price the paper

A simple vinyl for a small feature wall is one number. A grasscloth or a wide designer repeat for a dining room is another, and the material alone can run into the low thousands. Pattern, dye lot, and how much extra you need for the match move it.

We measure the wall and open the book before we quote. If you already own paper, bring the labels so we can see whether you have enough, or whether you should buy more from the same lot.

Hanging is a separate line when you want it. That work goes to a paperhanger we partner with. It is not an in-house workroom job.

## Why the book matters more than the video

Florida humidity is hard on the wrong paper. A bath paper that drinks steam will lift. A grasscloth in a steamy powder room looks tired in a year. We would rather sell you the vinyl that belongs there.

The housing stock is the other half. Bungalow plaster, block walls, and condos with heavy orange-peel spray are not the flat drywall in a product video. That is why we still come to the house. You pick the paper on the wall it will live on.
