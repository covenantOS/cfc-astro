## What We Hang in St. Pete Homes

Catalog paper and Florida-ready paper are often different rolls. We hang what belongs on the wall you actually have.

- **Designer and custom wallpaper.** Large repeats, murals, and papers you already bought from a showroom. We calculate drops before we cut.
- **Grasscloth and natural weaves.** Dining rooms, studies, and powder rooms where you want texture more than a printed pattern. Seams show on purpose. That is the look, and the hang has to be honest about it.
- **Vinyl and vinyl-coated wallpaper.** Baths, laundry rooms, rentals, and any wall that gets wiped. This is the paper that handles Florida humidity.
- **Feature walls.** One wall behind the bed or the dining table. Most of the calls we get start here, and it is usually the right place to start.
- **Commercial wallcovering.** Offices, waiting rooms, and restaurants that need a wipeable vinyl and a clean seam, not a hobby paper.

Peel-and-stick has its place on a short rental wall. We will hang it if that is what you brought. We will also tell you when a pasted paper will look better in a year.

## How Much Wallpaper Installation Costs Here

A feature wall in a standard bedroom often lands in the mid hundreds once prep and hanging are included. A full dining room in grasscloth or a paper with a wide repeat can run into the low thousands. Old paper that has to come off, walls that need skim-coating, and outside corners that are not square all add time.

We do not quote by the roll from a website. We measure the wall, check the match, and price the hang and the prep together. If you already own the paper, bring the labels.

## Why DIY Wallpaper Fails in St. Pete

The humidity is the first problem. Paste skins over, seams open overnight, and a paper that looked tight at 4 p.m. can peek at the joint by morning. The second problem is the housing stock. Bungalow plaster, block walls, and condos with a heavy orange-peel spray are not the flat drywall in the installation video.

We plumb the first drop, not the corner of the room, because that corner is rarely true. We also tell you when a wall should be lined or skimmed before any paper goes up. Skipping that step is how a $200 roll looks cheap.

## Prep, Removal, and the Wall You Already Have

Hanging is the visible half. Prep is the half that decides whether the paper stays flat.

If there is old wallpaper, we test a corner. Some paste releases. Some takes skim coat with it. We put that in the quote before we book the hang day. Fresh paint needs to be cured. Gloss needs to be dulled. Cracks get filled. Outlets get pulled so the paper tucks behind the plate instead of being cut short around it.

If the wall cannot take paper yet, we say so. Paint is cheaper than a hang that fails in August.
