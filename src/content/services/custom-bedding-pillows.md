## What Custom Bedding We Make

Custom duvet covers The most requested custom bedding item. Retail duvet covers are sized for standard mattress depths, which means if your mattress is thicker than average or if you have a mattress topper the cover doesn't fit properly. It shifts, it bunches, it never looks right. A custom duvet cover is made to your exact mattress dimensions, including the topper, so it fits and stays in place.

Custom pillow shams Pillow shams that actually fit your pillows. Standard shams are cut to fit standard pillow sizes, which means decorative pillows and non-standard pillows often don't have proper shams at all. We make shams to your exact pillow dimensions.

Decorative throw pillows Sized for your sofa, your bed, or your reading chair. Not just in the right size, but in fabrics you choose coordinated with your window treatments or your existing furniture. We make custom pillow covers in any size, any shape.

Accent pillows For beds, sofas, and built-in seating. Custom pillow forms with custom covers in fabrics selected for durability and look. If you've been settling for what's available at the home store because you couldn't find the right size, this is the answer.

Custom cushions Window seats, built-in benches, banquette seating, and other custom cushion needs. These are almost never standard sizes, which means they're almost never available ready-made. We measure and build exactly what fits your space.

## Why St. Petersburg Homes Are Different for Bedding

Florida changes what bedding needs to do.

Most bedding advice is written for temperate climates where humidity is occasional and summer means slightly warmer weather. St. Petersburg means high heat and high humidity for most of the year and homes that run air conditioning heavily in summer, which creates a different set of conditions than temperate-weather homes.

Here's what that means for your bedding choices:

Breathability matters. In Florida's climate, heavy quilts that work fine in Georgia are too warm for most of the year. Breathable fabrics quality cotton, linen, and linen-cotton blends perform better in Florida bedrooms. We help you choose materials that work with your AC usage and your sleep preferences.

Moisture resistance matters for some situations. If you have a pool room, a guest room that sits unused for months, or a room with inconsistent AC, certain fabric choices perform better than others. We talk through this at the consultation.

Coordinating with window treatments makes a room feel designed. One of the most underused design moves in St. Petersburg homes is coordinating the bedding fabric with the window treatments. The bed and the windows are the two biggest fabric elements in most bedrooms when they work together, the room feels intentional in a way that rarely happens by accident. We design both, which is why they tend to look better together.

## Custom Pillows vs. What You Can Buy at the Home Store

The pillow section at most home goods stores has one row of standard sizes and a wall of decorative pillows in whatever the season's color trend happens to be. If you need something in between a larger throw pillow for your sofa, a custom size for a built-in bench, a bolster for a daybed you're not going to find it there.

Custom pillows solve this. We make them to your specifications: size, shape, fill preference, and fabric.

Fill options: We discuss fill based on use. Firm support pillows for the sofa back. Soft fill for decorative pillows you actually rest against.Outdoor-rated fills for porches and covered outdoor spaces.

Fabric: Any upholstery-grade fabric works for a pillow cover which means your options go far beyond what's available in a pillow aisle. We help you choose fabrics that are appropriate for how you actually use the space.
