Roller shades have become one of the most requested window treatments in Snell Isle homes over the past several years, and for good reason. They clean up a window opening better than almost any other treatment available, they operate with minimal effort, and they can be integrated into almost any interior design aesthetic without imposing on it.

But roller shades are not all the same. The difference between a quality roller shade and a cheap one is immediately apparent once you've lived with both.

A quality roller shade has a properly balanced tube mechanism, the shade rolls up and down smoothly regardless of whether you've had it for six months or six years. A cheap one starts to roll unevenly and eventually the chain mechanism starts to slip. We've replaced enough of those cheap shades in Snell Isle homes to know exactly what that looks like.

Our roller shades come in three operational styles:

· Standard chain-operated, a continuous loop chain on one side, simple, reliable, and appropriate for most residential applications. The chain can be placed on the left or right side depending on your preference and furniture arrangement. · Motorized roller shades, integrated Somfy motorization that allows operation by wall switch, remote control, smartphone app, or voice command through Google Home or Amazon Alexa. The motor is concealed inside the tube, so from the outside the shade looks identical to a manual one. No exposed motors, no external battery packs, clean lines throughout. · Battery-powered motorized shades, for windows where running wiring isn't practical, we offer Somfy's battery-powered motorization system. The battery pack is concealed behind the headrail and typically needs charging two to three times per year, depending on usage. These are a practical solution for high windows and sliding glass doors where running low-voltage wire would require opening walls.

## Plantation Shutters for Snell Isle's Distinctive Architecture

Snell Isle's architectural character is unlike any other neighborhood in St. Petersburg. Mediterranean Revival homes with thick stucco walls, arched doorways, and tile roofs sit alongside custom contemporary builds with clean lines, expansive glass, and modern materials. A neighborhood with that range demands window treatments that are equally versatile.

Plantation shutters are the window treatment that works across almost all of these architectural styles without clashing with any of them. Clean-lined contemporary homes can use wide-louver shutters with a matte finish that complements the modern aesthetic. Mediterranean Revival homes look natural with narrower louvers, real wood construction, and a white or off-white paint finish that echoes the stucco and trim.

But Snell Isle's humidity and proximity to the water create real constraints that must inform any shutter purchase. Cheap shutters, even expensive-looking ones from certain online retailers, warp within one to two summers. The louvers twist, they don't close evenly, and the hinges start to show rust along the bottom edge. In a waterfront Snell Isle home, this failure mode is accelerated.

We specify coastal-grade faux wood shutters for waterfront applications and for any window that has direct exposure to humidity. These shutters are constructed from a moisture-resistant composite that won't warp, won't yellow, and won't rust. They look identical to real wood from a normal viewing distance, but they hold up in conditions that would destroy real wood shutters within a year.

For interior applications in climate-controlled spaces away from direct moisture exposure, we offer real wood shutters in select hardwoods with a full range of paint and stain finish options.
